CYCLE

Pour must stay shorter than the period, or the surface never gets to settle and the cup never returns to still.

—

Seek replays the cycle from a clean cup, so the spill and ripple state match the moment shown. Space also pauses.

COMPOSITION

Cup x/y are its place on the tray, in world units. The group head follows the cup, so the pour lands wherever the cup goes.

MACHINE

Drop the spout toward the rim to shorten the fall; the ripples wait for the stream to land either way.

STREAM
RIPPLES

Shading is the wave's slope, not its height — "catch" is how hard a crest takes the light. Lower "settle" makes the surface take longer to go still.

OVERFLOW

Dry speed is what returns the cup to clean before the next pour. Set it low and the streaks are still there when it starts again.

SHADES

1 is solid brightest and 0 is solid darkest; everything between dithers. Spilled coffee wants one of the two ends — mid grey makes it texture instead of liquid.

OUTPUT

Saved values replace the CFG block at the top of index.html. Space pauses.
