## What's here

No files have been filed yet. Three references were supplied in conversation
while the postcard was made, and they are described here so the judgement
survives even if the files are added later:

- **Two-frame ink sketch, "it overflows".** The controlling reference. Frame
  one: a still, full cup on the drip tray with drops coming down. Frame two:
  the same cup with the stream connected and coffee running over the rim,
  down the cup, across the tray and off its front edge. It fixes the *sequence*
  and the fact that the two states are the same composition — which is why the
  postcard is built as one still frame with a cycle inside it rather than as
  two scenes.
- **Two-panel dithered mockup of the same pair.** Works almost perfectly, and
  is closest on rendering: flat ink ground, near-solid cream masses, dither
  spent on the round forms and the shaded side. It reads as a screen-print,
  which is what the one-pass composite dither in `../index.html` is for. Where
  it was not followed: it is much closer in on the machine, and its overflow
  coats the whole cup — at this scale that buries the cup as a mass, so the
  postcard keeps the cup a flat lit shape and lets the spill be a few rivulets.
- **Photograph of the maker's own espresso machine.** Right for the object,
  clearly wrong as an image to reproduce: a colour photo, frontal, with a
  cluttered background of magnets and postcards and a busy windowsill. Useful
  only for the shapes — group head, portafilter and its long handle, the
  slotted drip tray — and deliberately stripped of everything else. Its yellow
  body and red cup are exactly the accent the brief rules out.
