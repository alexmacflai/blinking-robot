# Car circling a cloud-covered hill references

Supplied source material for the ticket #23 postcard. These files are context
only and are not runtime assets; see [`AGENTS.md`](../../../../AGENTS.md).

## Inventory

- [`IMG_2193.jpg`](IMG_2193.jpg) — supplied hand-drawn three-panel scene
  sketch. Panel one establishes the composition the postcard follows: a
  rounded hill between cloud layers, a house with a smoking chimney beside the
  road at the summit, a dashed road running over the top and down the front,
  and scalloped cloud masses across the base. Panel two is a closer study of a
  car cresting the summit, with an arrow marking the route up from the rear.
  Panel three crops tighter still on a car descending the front face toward
  the near cloud. See [`../brief.md`](../brief.md) for which panel sets the
  framing and what was not taken up.
- [`br-car.png`](br-car.png) — car shape and proportion reference. It specifies
  side/front construction, per-piece size variation, linked body/cabin width,
  and the engine-like vertical bob.
- [`br-carLights.png`](br-carLights.png) — headlight reference. It specifies
  headlights staying on and a projected cone of light landing on the road or
  hill.
- [`br-cloudsArc.png`](br-cloudsArc.png) — cloud-arc reference. It specifies
  that the cloud bank follows a broad curve rather than a flat horizontal
  edge.
- [`br-cloudsDistribution-mask.png`](br-cloudsDistribution-mask.png) — cloud
  depth/distribution reference. It specifies multiple cloud depths, with
  nearer clouds allowing the hill to show through their upper openings rather
  than reading as one solid foreground shape.
