# Cloud-drain warning sign references

Supplied source material for the ticket #15 postcard. These files are context
only and are not runtime assets; see [`AGENTS.md`](../../../../AGENTS.md).

## Inventory

- [`IMG_2180.jpeg`](IMG_2180.jpeg) — supplied hand-drawn scene sketch. Sets the
  portrait composition: a small sign high in frame, its pole descending into a
  continuous cloud mass that fills the lower frame, plus a plan-view spiral
  annotated "like a drain". Also carries two pictogram studies, of which the
  reaching hand is the one the ticket fixes.
- [`IMG_2181.jpeg`](IMG_2181.jpeg) — supplied hand-drawn scene and motion notes.
  Source of the pole-sway question and of three variants posed as open
  questions — flocks of birds, lightning, a crooked sign. See
  [`../brief.md`](../brief.md) for which were taken up and which were dropped.
