WIND

Negative reverses every layer, the deck, the birds and the star. The deck's seam is off-screen on both sides, so it just runs the other way.

LAYER HEIGHT
CLOUD DECK (inert)

Flat fill like the other banks. Shading darkens with depth below the surface — form, not grain.

The deck does not interact with the sails. Everything the mill does to cloud happens in the 5th cloud.

5th CLOUD

An invisible particle cloud sitting between L3 and the deck. Nothing is drawn until a sail touches it.

The rotor sweeps nearly the full width, so there is no upwind reservoir: once the medium already in frame is eaten, throughput is fixed at density × band height × drift speed. If it looks like spitting, raise DENSITY — output alone cannot beat that limit, it only over-feeds the incoming edge.

Launch is the kick off the blade face; fade is how long a revealed grain lasts before it is gone. Longer fade means more of them in the air at once for the same feed rate.

Gap held between a particle and the blade's drawn edge. It is a positional constraint, not a force — a particle can never be inside a blade at any clearance.
