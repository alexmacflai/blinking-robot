# Windmill brief

## Accent treatment

In 2-bit rendering, the moving cloud deck is the accent-bearing atmospheric
field: a broad, broken band behind and around the mill. It is intentionally
dominant enough to make the mill read as a neutral interruption; the 1-bit
image ignores that channel and retains its original tonal composition.

- A windmill turns through a wind-driven cloud sky.
- The sails reveal cloud material that was already present in apparently empty
  air.
- Cloud, depth, wind, and rotation must read as one relationship.
- Surface detail outside that relationship may be collapsed.
