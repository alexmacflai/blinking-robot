Flat fill like the other banks. Shading darkens with depth below the surface — form, not grain.

Scars ride downwind with the cloud. Heal is how long one takes to fade; soften stops it staying a punched-out hole.

Grab is how hard a grain matches the blade's own motion; squeeze is the outward shove that pushes it clear of the blade — the force that makes it look thrown, not carried.
