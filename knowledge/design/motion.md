# Motion

`[design]` `[motion]` `[coded-animation]` `[scene]`

Motion should expose a relationship in an otherwise mostly still world. It is
not decoration; it is part of the image's single visual idea.

## Behaviour

- Show motion through consequence: wind pushes, drags, distorts, and scatters.
- Rotation should affect nearby material rather than occur in isolation.
- Avoid generic motion lines unless they genuinely improve the read.
- Let movement be awkward, irregular, or slightly mechanical when that better
  serves the scene.
- Prefer small, legible changes over constant activity. Stillness is an active
  compositional choice.

## Light over time

Light may remain completely still, flicker, pulse, or vary with noise. Treat
that change as a graphic event, not realistic cinematic lighting logic.
