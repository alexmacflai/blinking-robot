# Gallery direction

`[design]` `[gallery]` `[art-direction]`

The gallery should frame postcards without competing with them: simple, sharp,
minimal, and typographically restrained. Mono fonts, hard edges, and an
absence of decorative softness support the project’s quiet, graphic character.

## Layout

Show postcards as a responsive grid with generous enough space for each work
to retain its frame. Explore cells governed by minimum and maximum widths or
by postcard aspect ratio. Use at most three columns on a large screen and one
on a small screen.

## Restraint

The gallery is a place to browse and watch, not a dashboard. Any filtering,
audio, palette, or development controls should stay visually subordinate and
should not obscure the default experience.

## Collection coherence

Explore a shared palette and pixel treatment for the gallery, while retaining
the possibility that a particular postcard needs a different visual decision.
This is direction, not yet a technical contract.
