# Postcard brief

`[design]` `[brief]` `[postcard]` `[scene]`

Use this compact brief in a postcard's README. It captures choices that change
from image to image and should not become project-wide art-direction rules.

## Template

```md
## Brief

- What is happening?
- Single visual idea:
- What matters most?
- What can be ignored or collapsed?
- What relationship or motion must read?
```

## Reference notes

When references are available, record examples that work perfectly, almost
work, and are clearly wrong. Add one or two notes explaining the judgement in
each case—not just its appearance. Keep these notes with the relevant brief
until a cross-postcard reference pattern emerges.
