# Postcard seeds

`[design]` `[postcard]` `[scene]` `[idea]`

Potential postcard scenes. A seed is not a committed postcard: it captures a
visual idea and the relationship or motion that must read, leaving composition,
tone, and rendering choices open until the work is selected.

## Coffee that never empties

**Image:** A full cup of coffee repeatedly drips or spills, then settles; waves
move across the surface and return again.

**Single visual idea:** Perpetual overflow without loss.

**Motion relationship:** Drip or spill → surface disturbance → settling →
repeat. The coffee level never changes and may remain implausibly at the rim.

**Open choices:** Camera, setting, degree of literal physics, and whether an
accent colour sharpens the image.

## Lonely drifting spaceship

**Image:** A small spaceship drifts through space forever, among distant space
clouds, stars, asteroids, planets, and galaxies.

**Single visual idea:** Smallness against an enormous, indifferent field.

**Motion relationship:** The ship drifts while distant material establishes
depth without becoming a spectacular space scene.

**Open choices:** Composition, pace, how abstract the background becomes, and
how to keep the image subdued rather than epic.

## Passing legs

**Image:** Several people's legs pass in formal trousers and shoes, as layered
silhouettes at different depths. The camera sits low and parallel to the floor.

**Single visual idea:** Anonymous movement rendered as a shifting wall of
silhouettes.

**Motion relationship:** Different walking rhythms create depth and awkward
overlap without requiring full bodies or a narrative event.

**Open choices:** Density, cadence, floor treatment, and whether the motion is
mechanical, hurried, or almost still.

## Appearing footsteps

**Image:** From directly above, many pairs of footsteps appear on a floor. Each
pair follows a different direction and fades over time.

**Single visual idea:** Traces of people without people.

**Motion relationship:** A new step appears, a path accumulates, then the path
fades; overlapping directions create a quiet, changing field.

**Open choices:** Surface material, footprint shape, density, and whether the
fading feels like wear, evaporation, or something more abstract.

## Endless dirt path

**Image:** From above, a person walks forever along a dirt path through grass,
trees, and occasional rocks. The locked camera follows them while the path
sways, branches, and never ends.

**Single visual idea:** Progress without arrival.

**Motion relationship:** The person remains in the frame while the world and
path continuously make room ahead; branches suggest choices without requiring
the walker to resolve them.

**Open choices:** How the path loops without revealing itself, level of
landscape abstraction, and whether the figure is isolated, mundane, or odd.
