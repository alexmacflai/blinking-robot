# Product

## Purpose

The home for Blinking Robot's durable product premise, lexicon, and uncommitted
directions for the gallery and the tools used to make postcards.

## Boundaries

- **In scope:** high-level experience ideas, authoring workflows, publishing
  concepts, and the reasons they may matter.
- **Out of scope:** decided technical architecture, implementation details, and
  build-ready requirements. Those graduate to `knowledge/decisions/`,
  `knowledge/engineering/`, or `knowledge/specs/` when ready.

## Local rules

Ideas are not commitments or a task list. Keep them concise, preserve their
open questions and tensions, and promote them only when a decision or a
build-ready specification is genuinely needed.
